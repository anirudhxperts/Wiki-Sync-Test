## Purpose

This guide explains how franchises use the corporate SOP wiki.

## Who should read this

- Franchise owners
- Office managers
- Field supervisors

## How updates work

1. Corporate edits the SOP in GitHub.
2. GitHub sends a webhook to each franchise site.
3. The wiki syncs automatically, usually within a minute.

> **Note:** Do not edit synced pages on your franchise site. They are read-only and will be overwritten on the next sync.
