title: Handling a New Prospect
---

## When a prospect arrives

Corporate routes prospects to your site automatically as a Lead.

## SOP

1. Contact the prospect within **24 hours**.
2. Update the Lead status to **Contacted**.
3. Log the call in the Lead timeline.
4. Convert to Customer once the first service is booked.

## Checklist

- [ ] Called within 24 hours
- [ ] Status updated
- [ ] Notes added
