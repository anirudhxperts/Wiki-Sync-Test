title: Stripe Setup
---

## Overview

Stripe credentials are broadcast from corporate. Franchises never enter secret keys manually.

## Steps

1. Confirm your franchise is enabled for Stripe on the corporate site.
2. Open **Stripe Settings** on your franchise site and verify the publishable key is filled.
3. Send a test payment link to an internal email.

| Field | Source | Editable |
|---|---|---|
| Publishable Key | Corporate broadcast | No |
| Secret Key | Corporate broadcast | No |
| Webhook Secret | Corporate broadcast | No |

See also: [Payment Links](payment-links.md)
